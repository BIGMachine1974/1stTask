## Step 1: Core messages (for approval)

### SecureMyOffer

**One-liner (every surface, adapted only for space)**

> SecureMyOffer is Home Closing Insurance for Canadian home sellers and their agents. It protects the seller when a buyer delays or defaults on a firm deal.

- What: Home Closing Insurance. Who: Canadian home sellers and their agents. Different: it covers the stretch between firm and closing, which a deposit doesn't.
- No guarantee language, no price, no deposit bond, national framing, seller named as the protected party.
- If D8 goes to "both products", this line gets redrafted.

**About version (2 to 3 sentences)**

> A firm offer isn't a closed sale. SecureMyOffer's Home Closing Insurance protects the seller if the buyer delays or defaults between the day the deal goes firm and closing day, covering costs a deposit alone won't. We work with REMAX and with agents across the country, and coverage must be in place at least 15 days before closing.

- If REMAX is named (D3), the surface carries the REMAX disclaimer. The live site footer already has it.
- "Across the country" stays off any surface that would read as covering Quebec. Quebec launches January 2027.
