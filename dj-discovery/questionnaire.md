# DJ Business Discovery Questionnaire

Oct 16, 2026 · @Wizard

## How to use

Nine sections, about 77 questions, built on the same structure as the Coach Mike and Dharma discovery forms. The answer type for each question is in brackets so you can map it straight to form fields.

- Section 3 (venues and vendors) feeds the venue pages directly.
- Sections 4 and 6 feed the automation build and the before-and-after case study numbers.
- Since you already know the business well, trim anything in Sections 1 and 2 you can answer yourself.

## Section 1: About the business

1. Full business name, and the name clients know you by. \[Short text\]
2. Best email and phone number, and your preferred way to be reached. \[Short text + choice: Email / Phone / Text\]
3. How long have you been DJing weddings and events, and how many events do you do in a typical year? \[Long text\]
4. Who is on the team today? (other DJs, MCs, techs, assistants) \[Long text\]
5. Which areas do you serve, and how far will you travel? \[Long text\]
6. Is this your full-time work or a side business? \[Choice: Full-time / Part-time / Seasonal\]
7. In one or two sentences, what do you want this project to do for the business? \[Long text\]

## Section 2: Services and ideal clients

1. Which services do you offer today? \[Checkboxes: Wedding DJ / MC / Ceremony audio / Cocktail hour music / Lighting and uplighting / Photo booth / Corporate events / Private parties / Live musicians / Other\]
2. Which service do you most want to sell more of? \[Long text\]
3. Which service or package makes you the most money? \[Short text\]
4. What are your packages and starting prices? \[Long text\]
5. Roughly what is your split between weddings, corporate and private events? \[Short text\]
6. Describe your ideal couple. (age, style, budget, music taste, type of wedding) \[Long text\]
7. Describe your ideal corporate client. (industry, event type, company size) \[Long text\]
8. Who are the clients you would rather not take? \[Long text\]
9. Do you do cultural or multilingual weddings? Which ones? \[Long text\]
10. Why do couples and companies pick you over other DJs? \[Long text\]
11. What do your best clients say about you? \[Long text\]

## Section 3: Venues and vendor partners

This section feeds the venue pages.

1. List every venue you have played, starting with the ones you play most. Add roughly how many events and the most recent one. \[Long text\]
2. Which 5 venues do you most want to be known for? \[Long text\]
3. For your top venues, share the insider details: where you set up, sound limits or curfews, load-in quirks, power, and tips couples should know. \[Long text\]
4. Are you on any venue's preferred vendor list? Which ones? \[Long text\]
5. Which venues would you like to break into? \[Long text\]
6. List the planners, photographers, videographers, florists, caterers and photo booth companies you work with most. Include company name and website or Instagram. \[Long text\]
7. Which of these vendors are you comfortable featuring and tagging on your venue pages? \[Long text\]
8. Do you have photos or video from past events at these venues? Who took them, and do you have permission to use them? \[Choice: Yes, with permission / Yes, need to ask / No + file upload + link\]
9. Any favourite event stories from these venues we could feature? (a big moment, a packed dance floor, a song that took over) \[Long text\]

## Section 4: Where bookings come from

This section sets the baseline for the referral rebuild.

1. Over the last 2 to 3 years, where have your bookings come from? \[Checkboxes: Planner referrals / Venue referrals / Other vendor referrals / Past clients and word of mouth / Google search / Instagram / Facebook / Wedding shows / Wedding directories / Other\]
2. Roughly what share of bookings came from your main referral partner? \[Choice: Under 25% / 25 to 50% / 50 to 75% / Over 75%\]
3. How has that changed in the last year? \[Long text\]
4. Which other planners, venues or vendors have sent you work, even once? \[Long text\]
5. Do you stay in touch with past couples and corporate clients? How? \[Long text\]
6. Do you offer anything for referrals today? \[Choice: Yes / No + details\]
7. Have you done wedding shows or bridal fairs? How did they perform? \[Long text\]

## Section 5: Goals

1. What do you need the most help with right now? Pick all that apply. \[Checkboxes: More bookings / Better-quality leads / Website / Social media content / Google reviews and visibility / Referral partnerships / Pricing and packages / Following up with inquiries / Admin and automation / Other\]
2. If we could fix only one thing first, what would it be? \[Long text\]
3. If we nailed this, what would be different in 12 months? \[Long text\]
4. How many weddings and corporate events a year would feel like a win? \[Short text\]
5. Rank what matters most right now. \[Ranking: More weddings / More corporate events / Higher prices / Less admin / More reviews / A stronger referral network\]
6. Any key dates coming up? (peak booking season, wedding shows, venue open houses) \[Long text\]
7. Where do you see the business in 2 to 3 years? \[Long text\]
8. How will you personally judge whether this worked? \[Long text\]

## Section 6: Sales process and admin

This section tells us which automations to build first.

1. Walk us through what happens from the first inquiry to a signed contract. \[Long text\]
2. Where do inquiries arrive? \[Checkboxes: Website form / Email / Phone / Text / Instagram DM / Facebook / Wedding directories / Other\]
3. How quickly do you usually reply to a new inquiry? \[Choice: Within an hour / Same day / Next day / Longer\]
4. Do you ever lose leads because you missed a call or replied late? \[Choice: Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Not sure\]
5. Roughly what share of inquiries end up booking? \[Choice: Under 25% / 25 to 50% / 50 to 75% / Over 75% / Not sure\]
6. When someone does not book, what is usually the reason? \[Long text\]
7. Do you follow up with couples who go quiet? How? \[Long text\]
8. How do couples share their music, must-plays, do-not-plays and timeline today? \[Long text\]
9. Which tools do you use for bookings, contracts, payments or planning? \[Long text\]
10. Do you ask for reviews after events? How many Google reviews do you have? \[Long text\]
11. Which tasks do you hate doing or lose the most time on? \[Checkboxes: Replying to inquiries / Quotes and contracts / Chasing payments / Planning calls / Music forms / Timelines / Asking for reviews / Social posts / Other\]
12. Roughly how many hours a week go to admin? \[Short text\]

## Section 7: Online presence

1. Do you own the domain? Who registered it and where is it hosted? \[Short text\]
2. List your social accounts and handles, and who posts on each. \[Long text\]
3. Do you have a Google Business Profile? Who has access to it? \[Short text\]
4. Are you listed on any wedding directories or vendor associations? Which ones? \[Long text\]
5. Do you capture content at events, like dance floor clips or crowd shots? Who films it? \[Long text\]
6. Are you comfortable on camera for short videos? \[Choice: Yes / Somewhat / Not really\]
7. Are you running any paid ads right now? Where, and roughly how much a month? \[Long text\]

## Section 8: Brand and voice

1. Pick three words that describe how you run an event. \[Short text\]
2. How should the brand feel? \[Checkboxes: High-energy / Elegant / Fun / Polished and professional / Modern / Classic / Laid-back\]
3. Do you have a logo, brand colours or fonts? \[File upload + link\]
4. Name 2 to 3 DJ companies, anywhere, whose brand you admire. \[Long text\]
5. Who are your main local competitors? \[Long text\]
6. Anything you never want associated with your brand? \[Long text\]
7. Should content be in English, French or both? \[Choice: English / French / Bilingual\]
8. Are there signature moments, songs or genres you are known for? \[Long text\]
9. What is your story? Why do you love doing this? \[Long text\]

## Section 9: Access and logistics

1. Which accounts can you give us access to? \[Checkboxes: Website / Domain / Google Business Profile / Instagram / Facebook / TikTok / YouTube / Email marketing / Booking or contract software / Calendar\]
2. Upload anything useful: logo, event photos, videos, testimonials, past client lists. \[File upload + link\]
3. Who makes the final call on approvals, and how fast can you usually review? \[Short text + choice: Same day / Within 2 days / Within a week\]
4. How do you want to communicate with us? \[Choice: Email / Text / Phone / Video call\]
5. What times work best for a 45 to 60 minute kickoff call? \[Long text\]
6. Do we have your permission to feature the business and results as a case study? \[Choice: Yes / Yes, without numbers / No\]
7. Anything else we should know, or anything you want to make sure we get right? \[Long text\]
