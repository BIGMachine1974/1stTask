## FAQ

What is an AI agent, and how is it different from ChatGPT?

An AI agent goes beyond a chatbot. While ChatGPT generates text responses in a conversation, a MuleRun AI agent takes action — it opens tools, follows multi-step workflows, and delivers completed results. Think of it as the difference between asking for advice and hiring someone to do the job.

What kinds of tasks can MuleRun automate?

MuleRun can automate a wide range of tasks including data analysis, content creation, email management, document processing, project coordination, monitoring dashboards, competitor research, social media scheduling, and much more. If it can be done on a computer, there's likely a MuleRun agent for it.

How does the always‑on agent actually work?

Your MuleRun agent runs on a dedicated computer that stays active around the clock — not a browser tab that closes when you log off. You can schedule workflows to run overnight, set up continuous monitoring, or assign tasks that execute on a recurring basis. Results are delivered to your inbox, Slack, or any connected tool when ready.

How does collective intelligence work? Is my data shared?

Collective intelligence means your agent benefits from anonymized workflow patterns and best practices learned across the platform — not from your private data. Your files, conversations, and business information remain strictly confidential. The system learns how to be more effective at tasks, not what your specific data contains.

Will the AI make mistakes? What if something goes wrong?

MuleRun agents are designed with safety checkpoints. For critical actions, the agent will ask for your approval before proceeding. You can set confidence thresholds, require human review for specific steps, and always have full visibility into what the agent is doing. If something doesn't look right, you can pause or redirect the workflow at any time.

Do I need technical skills or IT support to use MuleRun?

Not at all. MuleRun is designed for business users, not developers. You describe what you need in plain language, and the agent handles the technical execution. No coding, no complex setup, no IT tickets required.

How is MuleRun different from Zapier or Make?

Zapier and Make require you to build rigid, rule-based automations step by step. MuleRun agents understand context, adapt to changes, and handle complex multi-step tasks that would require dozens of Zaps. You describe the goal; the agent figures out the steps.

What does "self‑evolving" mean in practice?

Every time a workflow runs successfully on MuleRun, the platform learns from it. This means agents get better at handling similar tasks over time — faster execution, fewer errors, and smarter decision-making. The more the community uses MuleRun, the more capable every agent becomes.

Can I use MuleRun for overnight or after‑hours work?

Absolutely. That's one of MuleRun's core strengths. Your agent runs on a dedicated computer that operates 24/7. Assign a batch of tasks before you leave the office, and find completed results waiting for you in the morning. Set up monitoring alerts that trigger actions even while you sleep.

How do I get started?

Getting started is simple: sign up for a free account, describe the task you want to automate in the chat box, and hit Run Agent. MuleRun will match you with the right agent and walk you through the process. No credit card required for your first workflows.
